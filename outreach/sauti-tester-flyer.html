Try Sauti on your phone

Pakua. Sakinisha. Ongea.

Sauti is an AI assistant that understands Swahili, Sheng and English, by voice or text. It runs fully on your phone, with no internet once it's set up.

  1. Download the app

    Point your phone camera at QR code 1 and open the link. Download Sauti.apk.

  2. Install it

    Open Files → Downloads → Sauti.apk and tap Install.

    If Android blocks it, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.

    If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. It warns because this test build isn't on the Play Store yet.

  3. Add the model (once)

    Scan QR code 2. In Google Drive, tap ⋮ → Download to save sauti-base-gemma3n-e2b.litertlm (3.7 GB) to the phone. Use Wi‑Fi.

    In Sauti, open Settings → Import model file and pick it from Downloads, not from Drive.

    Keep Sauti open while it says "Bringing in the model file…". Copying and the first load take about 5 minutes.

  4. Talk to it

    Turn on flight mode to see it work offline. Tap the orb and speak Swahili, Sheng or English. It answers in the language you use.

Best on

Android phones with 6 GB RAM or more, and 8 GB free storage during setup (4 GB after). 4 GB RAM phones work but are slower.

Your privacy

Everything stays on your phone. You can turn off saved conversations, or delete them, in Settings.
